# Beta feature list

## VR weapons and aiming

- Controller-directed firearm aim with native damage rays and visible trails redirected from the visible muzzle.
- Per-weapon, per-hand grip calibration records.
- Two-hand long-gun support with independently calibrated primary and barrel hands.
- Left- and right-hand weapon presentation, one-hand firing, recoil handling, and magnetic waist storage.
- Independent custom akimbo for Pistol, Sawn-off Shotgun, Micro Uzi, and Tec-9, with separate hand intent, visible muzzles, traces, and effects while native ammo/damage/cadence remain authoritative.
- Controller-momentum grenade and Molotov throwing with custom visible flight and native GTA impact explosions, fire, damage, sound, and reactions.
- Dynamic front-waist weapon release placement with per-weapon recall.
- VR sniper scope with a repositioned scope lens and stabilized two-hand handling.
- Compact reticle and optional aim/combat-assistance settings.

## Physical melee

- Motion-contact attacks for bare fists, brass knuckles, and melee weapons.
- Independent left/right fist ownership and per-swing target deduplication.
- Native pedestrian damage and reactions where the resolved native path is supported.
- Conservative vehicle damage dispatch; advanced window/panel deformation and impact FX remain incomplete.

## Vehicles and cameras

- Free weapon aim from ordinary cars and supported motorbikes.
- Quest A fires in eligible vehicles while RT remains acceleration.
- Native left-hand steering presentation is retained where supported.
- Per-model camera offsets save and restore automatically.
- Native aircraft controls are preserved, including Hydra touch-D-pad support and short exterior-camera feedback for supported landing-gear changes.
- Bicycle-specific pedal/fire separation for BMX, Bike, and Mountain Bike.

## Controls and interface

- Left thumb-rest HUD tap, double-tap pin/unpin, hold modifier, and touch D-pad.
- Quest X quick weapon cycling on foot.
- Y+B pause chord and short camera-switch action.
- Phone-answer grip tap.
- HUD auto-hide and mission/pause visibility recovery behavior.
- Settings utility and Lua feature-flag interface.

## Presentation and quality

- Startup Quest 3 control layout artwork is provided separately for reference.
- Weapon/hand visibility logic designed for first-person VR.
- Body visibility defaults avoid the most disruptive torso clipping.
- Reduced muzzle-flash and VR texture PAKs from the original MIT-licensed project.
- VR-oriented cvars and profile defaults.

## Optional or experimental

- Grip calibration mode.
- Manual reload mode (disabled by default).
- Alternate left-handed input modes.
- Additional body visibility and clipping controls.
- Experimental diagnostics and calibration probes (disabled by default).
- Native Molotov throw A/B mode and throwable diagnostics (disabled by default).
